The seven courses

Friday and Saturday, for the whole table. US$75 a person, sample price.

  1. Course 1 of 7

    Akra and pikliz

    Hot from the fryer, to eat with your fingers.

  2. Course 2 of 7

    Lanbi, lime and cucumber

    Conch sliced thin and dressed at the table.

  3. Course 3 of 7

    Soup joumou

    A small cup of the soup Haitians share on the first of January.

  4. Course 4 of 7

    Pwason boukannen

    A piece of the whole snapper, straight off the coals.

  5. Course 5 of 7

    Diri ak djon djon

    Black mushroom rice, in one pot for the table.

  6. Course 6 of 7

    Kabrit boukannen

    Goat with epis, charred over wood.

  7. Course 7 of 7

    Pen patat

    Sweet potato pudding, and a short pour of clairin.
